Specific Rules per Banner
🏠
Hometop — WC/BAU Blocked from Slot #1-3
PLF BAU, WC, Mass Ads must use slot #4 or higher. Slots 1-3 are for SJBP/JBP/VIP.
🏠
Hometop #0 — New User Only
Hometop Banner 0 is exclusively for [PLF] New User programme.
🏠
Hometop — BAU Max 3 Days/Collection/Week
Same BAU collection max 3 days per week in Hometop.
🏠
Hometop — Adjacent Slots Cannot Share Category
Adjacent Hometop slots (#1/#2, #2/#3...) same day must have different L2 food categories.
🏠
Hometop — Individual Ads Max 3 Consecutive Days
[Win 1-4] and [Alacarte] in Hometop cannot run more than 3 consecutive days.
⭕
HTC — Same Brand Max 2 Consecutive Days
A brand cannot occupy HTC for more than 2 consecutive days in a row.
⭕
HTC — Coffee/Trà Sữa No Morning Weekend
Coffee & Trà Sữa brands cannot book HTC 0-8h or 8-16h slots on Saturday or Sunday.
🔍
Ongoing / Search Banner — PNS Only
These banner types are exclusively for [PNS] programme. No other programmes allowed.

📅 Rules by Day of Month
🚫
Double Day — Full Platform Block
1/1, 2/2, 3/3... 12/12. All slots reserved for platform campaign. No standard BD brand bookings.
🚫
15th Midmonth — Block Day
15th of every month is reserved for SPF campaigns. No BD brand bookings on this date.
⚠️
SPF Day — Platform Priority
ShopeeFood event days — check with ops lead. Brand slots may be significantly reduced.
⚠️
Thematic Campaign Dates
During Tết, Sale Seasons, Back to School etc. — brand booking slots are reduced and may require campaign alignment. Confirm with ops before booking.
